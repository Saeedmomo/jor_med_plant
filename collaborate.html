Collaboration portal

Contribute to the open database.

This project is built to grow. If you study Jordanian flora, you can add data — and be credited — helping turn this resource into a living national phytochemical map.

Chemistry

Phytochemical profiles

Isolated compounds, structures (SMILES / InChI) and the species they were found in.

Spectra

LC-MS & GC-MS results

Metabolite-profiling data for species that have never been analysed.

Taxonomy

Characterization studies

New species records, traditional-use documentation and bioactivity assays.

Submit a contribution

Fill in what you have — even a single species or compound helps. On submit, your entry is packaged into a pre-filled email (or a public GitHub issue) for review before it joins the open dataset.

No account required. Nothing is published automatically; every submission is curated and attributed before inclusion.
