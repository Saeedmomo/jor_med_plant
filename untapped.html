The research frontier

Untapped Jordanian flora.

Our literature survey was extensive but, by its nature, incomplete — many of Jordan’s species have never been chemically characterized. The plants below have little or no isolated-metabolite record and no published LC-MS / GC-MS profiling. They may represent untapped sources of novel bioactive secondary metabolites.

Why these gaps matter

🧬

Never chemically characterized

Several endemic species have zero reported secondary metabolites — their entire chemistry is unknown to science.

🔬

Missing LC-MS / GC-MS

Modern metabolite-profiling techniques have not been applied, leaving rich chemotypes undetected.

🌱

Open research opportunities

Each gap is a thesis project, a collaboration, or a discovery waiting for the next generation of scientists.
